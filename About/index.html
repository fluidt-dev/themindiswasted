The story

A mind spent where the fun is

This site started as a place to log games and spill thoughts after long sessions. It grew into a broader home for whatever refuses to leave my head — reviews, streams, tech rabbit holes, and the occasional movie night that turns into a write-up.

No corporate editorial calendar. No hype cycle cosplay. Just honest takes from someone who actually finishes (some of) the backlog and stays up too late doing it.
