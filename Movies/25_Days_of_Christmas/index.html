25 Days of Christmas

One holiday movie a day · 80s · 90s · 00s · 10s · 20s

The rules

One day. One reel. One take.

Twenty-five days, five eras, no Hallmark filler. Each film earns a spoiler-aware write-up after the credits — action Santas, haunted holidays, and feel-good finales.

  • 25 days · Dec 1–25, 2026
  • Eras: 80s · 90s · 00s · 10s · 20s
  • Rotating eras so no week feels stuck in one decade
  • Reviews drop after each watch
