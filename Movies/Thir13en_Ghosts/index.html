Verdict
Highly recommended

Steve Beck’s Thir13en Ghosts is one of my all-time favorite horror movies, which is why I gave it such a high rating. This is a rewatch — I have seen it at least 20 times. I just love the concept of a portal to hell powered by the dead, and each ghost has a trait that is required to make the machine work.

The Juggernaut

The Juggernaut has to be my absolute favorite of the ghosts.

My guilty pleasure

Arthur is a weak character, to say the least, but I think the ghosts make up for that character weakness. We all have that guilty pleasure movie, and this one is mine.
