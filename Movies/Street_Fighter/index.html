Verdict
Highly Recommended

I really enjoyed Street Fighter. Some laughs, lots of smiles, and a night at the movies that felt worth leaving the house for.

Casting and the fights

The character casting was on point, and the fight scenes were excellent. Those two things are what made this a fun watch, and the fights play even bigger with a theater crowd.

Soundtrack, action, and scale

The soundtrack, the action, and the effects are why this belongs on a big screen. Sound and scale do a lot of the work. A proper screen and speakers make this one hit harder than it would at home.

The story was the weak spot

The miss for me was the story. It was weak and confusing, and it could have been explained better. That’s the soft spot in an otherwise fun watch.

Final verdict — 8 / 10 popcorn buckets

Yes, see Street Fighter in theaters. The laughs, the smiles, the casting, the fights, and the sound and scale carried the night for me. A weaker, confusing story keeps it from a higher score, but this is still an easy yes if you want a good time on the big screen.
