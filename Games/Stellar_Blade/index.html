Stellar Blade, developed by Shift Up and released on April 26, 2024, exclusively for PlayStation 5, is a stylish action spectacle that arrives swinging. I’ve spent dozens of hours as Eve carving through Naytiba, and it’s an exceptional debut that earns five stars for combat feel, presentation, and sheer swagger.

Style Meets Substance

Visually, Stellar Blade is stunning. Eve’s character animations are fluid, and the costume designs are a delight to unlock and customize. The Naytiba enemies are grotesque yet fascinating, with designs that range from insect-like horrors to towering biomechanical beasts.

The combat is where Stellar Blade truly shines. Drawing inspiration from games like Sekiro and Bayonetta, it offers a perfect balance of challenge and accessibility. Eve’s moveset is a ballet of destruction—dodging, parrying, and unleashing Beta Skills feels incredibly satisfying. The parry system rewards precision, and the Beta Energy meter adds a layer of strategy to every encounter. Boss fights are intense and memorable, requiring you to master every mechanic to survive.

The story, while not groundbreaking, is surprisingly emotional. You play as Eve, a soldier sent to reclaim Earth from the Naytiba, but the narrative quickly delves into themes of humanity, sacrifice, and redemption. Side quests add depth to the world, and the soundtrack by Keiichi Okabe is a perfect complement, with haunting vocals and pulse-pounding battle themes.

Final Verdict — 5 Stars

If there’s a minor critique, it’s that the platforming sections can feel a bit clunky at times, and the open-world elements aren’t as expansive as some might hope. But these are small nitpicks in an otherwise stellar package. Shift Up has delivered a debut that’s not just good—it’s exceptional. For any PS5 owner who loves a challenge and a good story, this is a must-play. Five stars, without a doubt.
