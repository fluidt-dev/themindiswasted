LEGO Batman: Legacy of the Dark Knight has been a fun and rewarding drop into Gotham. I started on Steam the day it launched, and I’m still in the middle of it — not finished yet, but already locked into that classic LEGO loop of smash, build, and laugh.

Fun and rewarding

The game feels good to play. Progressing through levels, unlocking characters, and knocking out side stuff all scratch that rewarding LEGO itch. It’s the kind of adventure that keeps you hopping back in for “one more area” without feeling like a chore.

Co-op is the highlight

What I really enjoy is the co-op. That’s always been one of the best parts of LEGO games for me — splitting the screen (or the couch), bouncing between characters, and solving the world together. Legacy of the Dark Knight keeps that energy alive, and it’s a big reason this one stays on the stick.

Status — In progress

Still playing, not complete. More notes (and a star rating) will land once I’ve finished the run. For now: fun, rewarding, and best shared in co-op.
