Started Grand Theft Auto VI on Monday, 11/23. So far this game is subpar for a GTA — it feels much more limited and reined in compared to GTA 5, and the early hours haven’t won me over.

More limited than GTA 5

The biggest feeling right now is how constrained it is next to GTA 5. The sandbox that used to feel wide open feels smaller and more locked down. For a series built on chaos and freedom, that reined-in vibe is a rough first impression.

Car theft and the wanted system

I’m not a complete fan of the new car-stealing mechanics. It’s a change that gets in the way of the old GTA flow more than it adds to it. On top of that, the police wanted system feels scaled for casual players — softer, safer, and less tense than what I want from this franchise.

The story so far

The story up to this point has been pretty lame. Nothing has hooked me yet, and the missions haven’t carried the early game the way a Rockstar open world usually does. Still early, but the narrative isn’t doing me any favors.

Final verdict — 2 Stars

Early take, still in progress: 2 out of 5 stars. Limited feel vs GTA 5, car theft changes that miss for me, a casual-leaning wanted system, and a lame story so far. Maybe they’ll fix some of this in an upcoming update — I’ll keep an eye on patches and revisit if things improve.
